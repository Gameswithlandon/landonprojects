On the staff side

Ranks

A rank is a named position tied to a Discord role, with its own level. A lower level cannot act on a higher one.

Hire and fire

Hiring opens a staff profile, gives the rank, and grants the primary staff role. Firing takes them back.

Leave

Hired staff request leave with a reason and dates. Someone with leave approval can approve, deny, or end it.

Tickets

Members open a private thread from a panel. A linked Hub gets a matching triage thread, and messages typed on either side are mirrored. Claim, notes, close, and transcripts stay with staff.

Strikes

Ranks that can issue strikes can view a hired member’s record, add a strike with a reason, or remove an active one. The change posts to the discipline log.

Logs and settings

Per-kind log channels, ranks, the ticket panel, auto-close, and an alert role all live in /settings.

What it hears

/setup /staff hire /loa request /ticket claim /ticket summary

Setup, staff, leave, and settings stay on slash commands, buttons, and forms. Message content is read only inside open ticket threads so those conversations can be mirrored. Other channels stay unread.
