On the staff side

Ranks

A rank is a named position tied to a Discord role, with its own level. A lower level cannot act on a higher one.

Hire and fire

Hiring opens a staff profile, gives the rank, and grants the primary staff role. Firing takes them back.

Leave

Hired staff request leave with a reason and dates. Someone with leave approval can approve, deny, or end it.

Commands, buttons, and forms. That is the whole interface. Ordinary channel messages stay unread.
